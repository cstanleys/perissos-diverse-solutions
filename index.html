WHO WE ARE & WHAT WE DO

Perissos Diverse Solutions has been operational in the West African Market for 35 years, excelling in various business verticals. The Perissos Group has operations spread across Africa, the Middle East, the United Kingdom, India, Indonesia, and Singapore. We are a System Integrator with an objective to provide innovative solutions to West African countries, including Nigeria, Ivory Coast, Ghana, and Togo.

Perissos Diverse Solutions has an in-house and dedicated team for delivering all kinds of audio-visual solutions and services. In a short span of time, we have completed several multifaceted projects on schedule across various territories, driven by our business philosophy of dedication, professionalism, and efficiency. Our focus has been on audio-visual system development and marketing since our establishment, with full competency in resource distribution and control in the value-added chain.

To bring more edge to our solutions and to facilitate our customers and partners, we have partnered with world-renowned OEMs. This enables us to deliver the latest technologies to our customers in a cost-effective manner.
